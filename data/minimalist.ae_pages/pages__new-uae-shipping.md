## UAE SHIPPING

After your payment is verified, We dispatch the order within the same business day. This does not include weekends or holidays. Purchases made after 3 PM GST will not be shipped out until the next business day. Incase of Same Day Delivery, orders need to be placed before 11 AM GST. If you order after 3 PM GST on a Saturday, your order will likely be shipped out on the following Monday. Please refer to the chart below for rates/times.

### SHIPPING RATES & DELIVERY

| SERVICE | COST | DELIVERY TIME |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah | FREE for orders over 500 AED   20 AED for orders below 500 AED  25 AED for COD orders below 500 AED | ARAMEX DELIVERY TIME: 2-4 BUSINESS DAYS |
