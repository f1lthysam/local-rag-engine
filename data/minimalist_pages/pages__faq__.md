## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

### Shipping & Payment Information

- #### When Will My Order Ship?

It depends on where you are. Orders processed here will take 1-3 business days to arrive. Overseas deliveries can take anywhere from 3-7 days. Delivery details will be provided in your confirmation email.

- #### Will I Have To Pay International Taxes & Duties?

Your order may be subject to import duties and taxes, which are levied once a shipment reaches your country. The general amount for the duties and taxes fee is about 20% of the dollar amount of the merchandise. However, this is just a general guideline and may vary depending on the country to which the order was shipped. You should contact your customs office for specific amounts and percentages.

MINIMALIST cannot control and is not responsible for any duties/taxes applied to your package. You will be responsible for paying additional charges for customs clearance. Customs policies vary widely from country to country; please contact your local customs office for further information. Note, in rare occasions custom agents may delay delivery of some packages.

- #### How are taxes and duties calculated?

Duties Delivered Unpaid (DDU) Orders: As the customer, you are liable for all import duties, customs and taxes levied. Payment of these is required to release your order from customs on arrival.

### Order Information & Concerns

- #### I Need To Change Something On My Order. How Can I Do That?

If you need to change or cancel your order, please [contact us](mailto:support@minimalist.ae "mailto:support@minimalist.ae") immediately. Once our warehouse has processed your order, we will be unable to make any changes.

- #### My Order Status Says "Unfulfilled". What Does That Mean?

"Unfulfilled" just means that we successfully received your order! Once your order is shipped, you'll receive a tracking number and your order status will change to "Fulfilled."

- #### Why Was My Order Canceled?

If your order was unexpectedly cancelled, chances are that our fraud filter marked your order as fraudulent. If you are certain that that is not true, please order again with a Paypal account. If you do not have Paypal, you can easily sign up at www.Paypal.com

- #### Can I change my address?

Yes we can change the address if the item was not Fulfilled but in case we handover the order to a courier company you may contact them to correct the address.

- #### Can I add or remove items from the order that I just placed?

Contact the [**Customer support**](https://www.minimalist.ae/pages/contact_us "Customer support") to remove & add items.

### WATCHES

- #### What type of movement is used?

Our watches feature reliable quartz or hybrid chronograph movements for accurate timekeeping.

- #### Are your watches water-resistant?

Yes, our watches are water-resistant, designed to withstand everyday splashes and light rain. Please check the product details for specific water resistance levels.

- #### What materials are your watches made from?

Our watches are crafted from premium stainless steel with sapphire crystal glass for scratch resistance and durability.

- #### Are the straps adjustable or replaceable?

Yes, most of our watch straps are adjustable and replaceable. Please refer to the product description for compatibility.

- #### Why are the hands on my chrono subdials not pointing straight up?

This is actually a very simple fix, please see the instructions below.

**If the far left sub-dial is not centered, you will need to press the correct button (reference above) continuously. The stopwatch minute hand (far left dial) is synchronized with stopwatch second hand (middle dial).**

Hope this makes sense, if you have ANY issues please don't hesitate to ask.

- #### How do I replace my battery?

All of our watch batteries can be found at any electronic store, as well as most watch repair or jewelry shops. Any watch repair shop or Jeweler should be able to assist you with the battery replacement. We don't suggest replacing the battery, as it requires some special tools and you could damage the watch.

- #### What type of battery is used?

Our watches use either SONY SR626SW or SONY SR621SW. Please [contact us](/pages/new_contact "new_contact") for further details.

- #### Do your watches come with a warranty?

Yes, all our watches come with a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects.

- #### How do I maintain my watch?

Clean your watch gently with a soft cloth and avoid exposing it to extreme temperatures or strong chemicals.

- #### Do you offer free gift wrapping?

Yes, we offer complimentary gift wrapping for all our watches.

- #### Do you offer watch customization or engraving?

Currently, we do not offer customization, but stay tuned for updates on this feature.
