## A vibrant harmony of floral and warmth

## A vibrant harmony of floral and warmth

This scent begins with the romantic bloom of rose, tuberose, magnolia, and violet. It moves through an airy heart of jasmine, muguet, orris, and basil, before unfolding into a soft, lasting blend of amber, leather, caramel, and musk. A fragrance that feels both familiar and new with every wear.

## Perfume Notes

## Perfume Notes

The Secret Rose Parfum opens with notes of rose, tuberose, magnolia, and violet. It reveals its depth with a soft floral bouquet of muguet, jasmine, orris, and basil. The base unfolds into warm, creamy, and musky notes of dry amber and leather, grounded by the subtle sweetness of caramel and musk.

## Art In A Bottle

## Art In A Bottle

Secret Rose is housed in a frosted red bottle, elegantly contrasted with golden details on the regal crown and label. The rich red tone reflects a sense of passion, beauty, and timeless elegance, blending seamlessly with the bottle’s contemporary arabesque heritage design.
