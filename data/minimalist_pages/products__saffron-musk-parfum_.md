## A Luxurious Eid Fragrance

A fragrance of depth and distinction, crafted exclusively for Eid. Saffron Musk is a luxurious blend of rich saffron, warm amber, and velvety musk. It opens with a fresh burst of bergamot, adding an unexpected brightness before settling into a deep, musky base. A modern yet timeless scent, perfect for gifting this Eid.

## Perfume Notes

## Perfume Notes

Saffron Musk opens with the rich, golden warmth of saffron, infused with the vibrant zest of bergamot for a bright, citrusy touch. At its heart, warm amber and earthy patchouli envelop the fragrance in deep, comforting warmth. Finally, velvety musk and refined vetiver leave a smooth, long-lasting trail.

## Art In A Bottle

## Art In A Bottle

Saffron Musk is encased in a frosted amber bottle, elegantly contrasted by golden details on the regal crown and label. The warm amber hue reflects the richness of saffron and the festive spirit of Eid, seamlessly blending with the bottle’s contemporary arabesque heritage design.
