## LEATHER CARE

Use a soft dry cloth to remove any dust or dirt

Avoid using wipes containing alcohol, fragrance, or dye.

Keep away from water and perfumes

Keep in a cool and dry place, away from direct sunlight

Cover the bag in a dust bag to avoid scratches

Avoid placing sharp objects such as keys and jewelry

Leather stretches, so avoid overfilling to maintain its shape

## Jewellery Care

Store Jewellery In A Soft Lined Box. Do Not Store Jewellery Over-Lapped.

Please Remove Jewellery Before Washing Hands. Avoid Contact With Water.

PROTECT YOUR JEWELLERY FROM SHARP BOWS AND SCRATCHES

AVOID DIRECT CONTACT WITH PERFUME, BODY LOTIONS OR OTHER CHEMICALS.

ALL STONES ARE HAND SET INTO THE JEWELLERY. EACH PIECE IS UNIQUE AND MAY VARY IN COLOUR.

AVOID WEARING WHILE CLEANING OR PERFORMING SPORTS ACTIVITIES

## WATCH CARE

WIPE THE WATCH WITH A SOFT, LINT-FREE CLOTH TO REMOVE DUST, FINGERPRINTS, AND MOISTURE

CLEAN METAL BRACELETS REGULARLY TO PREVENT THE BUILDUP OF DIRT AND OILS., USE A DRY SOFT BRUSH TO CLEAN THE LINKS

TRY TO KEEP YOU WATCH AWAY FROM MAGNETS

DO NOT OPEN TO REPAIR YOUR WATCH ALL BY YOURSELF

Store your watch in the watch box when not in use to avoid scratches

Avoid contact with chemicals such as cleaning supplies and perfumes

## SUNGLASSES CARE

Gently cleanse your eyewear with soap and water.

Keep your eyewear in a case and store it in a dry place with a temperature range on 10 degree C to 35 degree C

Avoid exposure to direct sunlight, artificial UV radiation and any shocks that could break or damage your eyewear.

Be careful to never place the lenses face down

Never use solvents, alcohol or abrasive cloths
