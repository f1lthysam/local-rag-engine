## Questions? We Have Answers.

- ### What materials are your watches made from?

Our watches are crafted from premium stainless steel with sapphire crystal glass for scratch resistance and durability.

- ### How do I replace my battery?

All of our watch batteries can be found at any electronic store, as well as most watch repair or jewelry shops. Any watch repair shop or Jeweler should be able to assist you with the battery replacement. We don't suggest replacing the battery, as it requires some special tools and you could damage the watch.

- ### What type of battery is used?

Our watches use either SONY SR626SW or SONY SR621SW. Please contact us for further details.

- ### How do I maintain my watch?

Clean your watch gently with a soft cloth and avoid exposing it to extreme temperatures or strong chemicals.

- ### Are your watches water-resistant?

Yes, our watches are water-resistant, designed to withstand everyday splashes and light rain. Please check the product details for specific water resistance levels.

- ### What type of movement do your watches use?

Our watches feature reliable quartz or hybrid chronograph movements for accurate timekeeping.

- ### Do your watches come with a warranty?

Yes, all our watches come with a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects.

- ### Are the straps adjustable or replaceable?

Yes, most of our watch straps are adjustable and replaceable. Please refer to the product description for compatibility.

- ### Do you offer free gift wrapping?

Yes, we offer complimentary gift wrapping for all our watches.

- ### Do you offer watch customization or engraving?

Currently, we do not offer customization, but stay tuned for updates on this feature.

Clean your watch gently with a soft cloth and avoid exposing it to extreme temperatures or strong chemicals.
