## Our Story

It started with an idea: To minimize and de-clutter our lives, creating quality and well-designed products that we wanted ourselves.

### "Design defines us. Our story is about designing products for people who find beauty in simplicity, and away from distractions by focusing on truly important things in life."

**AHMAD YOUSUF **\
CEO | FOUNDER

### OUR MISSION

The Minimalist Store was founded on the belief that style should be kept simple and that great products could be made by delivering a minimalist & sleek look.

### "We aim to deliver products that are simple, clean and beautiful."

\

Minimalist’s in-house design team seamlessly blends Arab style and heritage with luxurious materials and exquisite details - you can feel the care and attention in each timepiece radiate from your wrist.

- 1/ Prioritize
- 2/ Strip out all distractions
- 3/ Minimize
